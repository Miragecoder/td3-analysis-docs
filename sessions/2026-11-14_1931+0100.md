## Trees and tile placements

- Traced `model_place()` in the enhanced port: tile models can include child
  sprite records with IDs and local X/Z/Y positions.
- The game rotates child positions with the map cell and places them relative
  to the tile. Sprite IDs at or above `0x12` are conditionally omitted using a
  vegetation seed.
- The current TypeScript mesh extractor skips these child records, explaining
  why top-down tile previews can omit in-game vegetation. Independent 3D
  scenery also comes from the map entity table.
- Added the findings and a note about the remaining sprite-ID/image mapping
  work to `docs/map-entities-and-gameplay.md`.

## Reverse-course feasibility
