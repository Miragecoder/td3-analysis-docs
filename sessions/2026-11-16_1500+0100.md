# LST editor fixes, SCENE03 LST export, and PLAYDISK count diagnosis

Follow-up fixes and tooling to make SCENE03 course experiments loadable
in-game.

## Scene LST editor fixes

- Fixed route name reading/writing to treat route slots as fixed
  12-character, space-padded fields: values are trimmed for editing, up to
  12 characters are accepted, and written slots are space-padded to match the
  original LST format (resolving leading-whitespace display and
  "Route names must be shorter than 12 characters" apply errors).

## SCENE03 LST generation and export

- Added best-effort SCENE03 LST generation from `SCENE01.LST` during the
  Pacific inversion experiment by rewriting scene archive-directory hashes
  from `SCENE01*` to `SCENE03*` resource names.
- Kept LST string editing/reset/export available in inversion mode and
  export the generated working copy as `SCENE03.LST` alongside
  `SCENE03.DAT` and `SCENE03P.BIN`.

## PLAYDISK active count diagnosis and generator clarity

- Diagnosed why a registered `SCENE03` could appear in the launcher/play
  disk course list but not in the in-game course selection: `PLAYDISK.DAT`
  stores active counts as `DS_cars_on_disk` at `0xAE` and
  `DS_scenes_on_disk` at `0xAF`; the in-game selection uses
  `DS_scenes_on_disk`, so adding a third scene requires setting byte `0xAF`
  to `3` (in addition to placing `SCENE03` in a scene slot and providing
  matching files).
- Clarified the PLAYDISK generator by exposing **Cars on disk (0xAE)** and
  **Scenes/courses on disk (0xAF)** as labeled fields instead of generic
  tail bytes.
