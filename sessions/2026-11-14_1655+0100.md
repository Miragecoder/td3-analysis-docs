# Course-data walkthrough

**When:** 2026-11-14 16:55 (+01:00)

## Summary

- Confirmed both scene LSTs specify five legs/maps and include route metadata:
  the first 20 labels represent four route choices for each of five legs.
- Each leg's `0x2137`-byte map has a 32 × 16, 2-byte-per-cell tile grid,
  secondary entity data, a 13-array entity table, and color remap tables.
- Course DATs share scene tile banks across their five maps. `SCENE01` uses
  `DATAB.DAT`'s shared scene-object bank; `SCENE02` has its own scene-object
  bank. Each course has a billboard sprite bank and three sub-scene
  palette/backdrop pairs.
- The runtime day/dark mode corresponds to map byte `0x35` (`DS_colour_mode`
  in the C port). Its values vary by leg: Pacific `0,1,1,0,0`; Cape Cod
  `1,0,0,1,1`. The neighboring byte `0x36` is a different sky-seed selector
  in the format notes and is zero in all ten sampled map blobs.
- Selectable/opponent `.POB` car models are loaded separately from the
  course's scene-object bank, so not every vehicle mesh is course-specific.
