## Highway shield sign construction

User asked whether highway shield signs are unique objects or a single model with customizable numbering.

- Checked raw models in `Game/DATAB.DAT` (object bank at `0x32F1A`):
  - `0x3c` (Highway 1): `npoly=4, npoints=9, nsprites=0`, ~90 bytes
  - `0x3d` (Highway 101): `npoly=9, npoints=15, nsprites=0`, ~166 bytes
- Conclusion: these are **separate 3D object models**, not one parameterized shield model; the number is baked into geometry.
- Contrast: speed signs `0x08` (55) and `0x1c` (35) share the same structure (`npoly=12, npoints=18`, 208 bytes) but differ in 32 bytes - distinct entries with similar layout.
- Generating new number models is possible in principle but needs a specialized deep-dive: fully document object model format/write path, identify digit/face geometry, and build/validate a model authoring/export workflow.
