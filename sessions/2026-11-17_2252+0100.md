# Enhanced port: extended draw distance and random opponent cars

Checked recent additions in `repos/testdrive3-enhanced-analysis` (commit
`a9a4b97`, merging `drawdistance`/`e740760` and `rand-opponent-cars`/`1bbfc40`,
`3b55087`) and logged the two notable gameplay/rendering features.

## Extended draw distance / far ring (`e740760`, PR #1 lineage)

- Draw distance is extended to cover the whole wrapping 32×16 leg map, with a
  default of 18 cells (`--draw-distance`, 0–18; 0 = the game's original
  window). This goes beyond the original 3/6/10-cell visibility.
- The far ring uses 32-bit vertex offsets (instead of the game's 16-bit x4
  offsets) and selects cells, objects, cars, and sprites by their nearest image
  across the map's seams, so content across the toroidal wrap is placed
  correctly relative to the camera.
- Cars that the game culls at its own draw distance are carried into the
  enhanced far ring with their live motion and heading, avoiding pop-in as the
  landscape renders farther out.
- `--far-lod 1` restores the original reduced far models for vehicles and some
  buildings (off by default: the enhanced view keeps full-detail models at every
  distance). The launcher exposes a matching "Use original far models at
  distance" checkbox.
- Main implementation touchpoints: `src/enhanced/enh_far.c` (far ring
  construction/placement), `src/enhanced/enhanced.c`, rendering/object paths,
  `src/portcfg.*`, `src/main.c`, launcher options/UI, and docs
  (`CHANGELOG.md`, `ENHANCED.md`, `README.md`).

## Random opponent cars (`1bbfc40`, PR #2)

- The two computer opponents are chosen randomly from the other cars installed
  on the play disk (including expansion cars), once per course, and the same two
  cars race all legs of that course. This replaces the original behavior where
  rivals were derived from the player's car slot per leg and expansion cars were
  never used by the computer.
- On by default. `--random-rivals 0` (and a launcher checkbox) restores the
  original three-car selection behavior. When only two cars are installed, both
  opponents still share the single other car as before.
- Main implementation touchpoints: `src/game/flow_race.c`
  (`race_opponents_pick()` selecting/storing `DS_opp1_slot`/`DS_opp2_slot` once
  per course, with `stage_load_objects()` reusing them), `src/game/flow.c` /
  `flow.h`, `src/portcfg.*`, `src/main.c`, launcher options/UI, and docs.
