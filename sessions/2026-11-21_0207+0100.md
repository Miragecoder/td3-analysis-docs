# SCENE01/SCENE03 horizon (sky gradient) investigation and course-editor Sky & horizon UI

## Context / question
User reported that in `SCENE01` (5 legs) legs 2–3 are daytime and legs 1,4,5 are night, but leg 5 has a darker horizon than the other night legs. Asked what controls this and whether it can be implemented in the track editor. Follow-up: whether this is original DOS data (not enhanced-only), why `SCENE03` leg 5 (daytime in the flipped/inverted set) shows a weird dark horizon bar, whether night legs 1/4/5 each have unique sky/horizon color sets, and whether the editor needs more than a day/night toggle (with a visual preview).

## Findings (confirmed from shipped data / repo docs & code)

### Per-leg map-side controls (original data)
- `map+0x35` (`byte_285B3`): day/dark color-mode (`0` = dark/night, `1` = day/light). Affects world/lighting/remap treatment.
- `map+0x2E..0x31` (`byte_285AC..285AF`): sky/horizon base/companion seed bytes (procedural in-race sky/gradient inputs).
- `map+0x32..0x33` (`word_285B0`): gradient seed selecting a remap-table index `idx = (low&0x0F)|((high&0x0F)<<4)` (all sampled SCENE01/SCENE02 legs use `0x0203` → `0x23`).
- `map+0x1F27 + idx*2`: 256-entry per-map color/remap table entry (paired values) used by the sky/horizon gradient path. Dark mode applies its remap/darkening treatment.

Checked `Game/SCENE01.DAT` per leg (offsets `0x10240,0x1A1CC,0x1C319,0x21565,0x236B2`):

| Leg | mode | `0x2E..0x31` | fog | idx | `remap[idx]` |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 night | `03,08,74,14` | `0x0203` | `0x23` | `0x6767` |
| 2 | 1 day   | `0B,08,79,19` | `0x0203` | `0x23` | `0x6868` |
| 3 | 1 day   | `0B,08,79,19` | `0x0203` | `0x23` | `0x6868` |
| 4 | 0 night | `09,08,72,13` | `0x0203` | `0x23` | `0x6666` |
| 5 | 0 night | `09,08,70,11` | `0x0203` | `0x23` | `0x6565` |

- **SCENE01 leg 5 darker horizon:** not a separate extra-dark flag. All night legs share mode `0` and idx `0x23`, but leg 5 has different seeds (`09,08,70,11` vs leg 4 `09,08,72,13`, leg 1 differs further) and a lower remap value (`0x6565` vs `0x6666`/`0x6767`). Per `docs/course-data-structure.md` the LST also selects different backdrop groups per leg (SCENE01 suffixes/groups `1,3,3,3,5`), so leg 5 differs in backdrop selection as well as map-side gradient values.
- **All three night legs differ:** leg 1 vs 4 differ in seeds and remap (`6767` vs `6666`) and backdrop group (1 vs 3); leg 4 vs 5 differ in `0x30,0x31`, remap (`6666` vs `0x6565`), and backdrop group (3 vs 5).
- **SCENE02** also varies per leg (e.g. modes `1,0,0,1,1`, differing seeds, remap `5757/5757/5656/5757/5757` at idx `0x23`), showing scene-specific per-leg values.

### SCENE03 leg 5 dark-bar artifact (explained)
`Game/SCENE03.DAT` matches the reversed/inverted+lighting-toggled transform (preserving per-leg offsets via canonical night↔day preset deltas, as in `invertPacificCourse`/`toggleMapLighting`):

| SCENE03 leg | mode | `0x2E..0x31` | `remap[idx]` | derived from SCENE01 leg |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 1 day   | `0B,07,77,17` | `0x6666` | 5 (night `09,08,70,11`,`6565`) |
| 2 | 1 day   | `0B,07,79,19` | `0x6767` | 4 (night `09,08,72,13`,`6666`) |
| 3 | 0 night | `09,09,72,13` | `0x6767` | 3 (day `0B,08,79,19`,`6868`) |
| 4 | 0 night | `09,09,72,13` | `0x6767` | 2 (day `0B,08,79,19`,`6868`) |
| 5 | 1 day   | `05,07,7B,1A` | `0x6868` | 1 (night `03,08,74,14`,`6767`) |

SCENE03 leg 5 is daytime with remap `0x6868` but seeds `05,07,7B,1A` (SCENE01 leg 1’s non-canonical night seeds shifted by the night→day delta, not canonical day `0B,07,79,19`). That preserved per-leg deviation in the original sky/gradient inputs explains the odd dark horizon bar — original data/parameterization carried across the flip, not an enhanced-only fog feature.

### Clarifications (mental model)
- `0x35` is the in-race day/night (dark/light) color-mode/lighting treatment; `0x2E..0x33` + indexed `0x1F27` are the per-leg procedural in-race sky/horizon gradient parameters (tunable per leg, not a global named palette pair).
- `landA`–`landF` / per-leg `<scene><d>.COL,BLZ,ALZ` sets are **UI/score/leg-banner/menu images** (e.g. `leg_banner_draw` in `flow_scores.c`, `scene_select_draw` in `flow_menus.c`), not in-world 3D backdrops; editor correctly labels them “Scene UI bitmaps”.
- The in-race ground/“grass” fill below the horizon is a separate procedural fill (`sky_ground_draw` / ground color-pair path); its exact per-leg map-data source was not conclusively traced here (so not asserted to be the same field/value as the sky-gradient remap entry).

## Implementation (course editor: `td3-analysis-docs/repos/testdrive3-course-editor`)
Made the per-leg horizon/gradient editable and visual (map-side, scene/leg-specific):

- **`src/shared/course-map.ts`**: added helpers for sky seeds (`0x2E..0x31`), fog/gradient seed (`0x32..0x33`), fog index, remap entries (`0x1F27`), canonical presets and night↔day delta toggle. `writeMapMode` now sets `0x35` only (non-destructive) instead of forcing canonical seeds.
- **`editor.html`, `src/browser/editor.css`, `src/browser/editor.ts`**: replaced simple Lighting control with a **Sky & horizon** panel — per-leg mode and gradient editing with live preview (updates while editing), labeled Sky/Horizon swatches, simplified mode switching (switch with “keep horizon variant” delta by default / reset to canonical colors), scene-aware “match another leg” chips, apply/reset, and copy of horizon settings within the scene. Exposes `0x35`, `0x2E..0x31`, `0x32..0x33`, derived index, and indexed `0x1F27` remap entry (values stored in the leg’s map data).
- **`spec/course-inversion.test.ts`**: updated fixture to apply canonical seeds explicitly after `writeMapMode` behavior change.

**Not yet wired:** per-leg LST palette/backdrop selector for the COL/BLZ/ALZ (UI image set) choice — field/encoding not yet modeled in `scene-lst.ts`/editor; left for follow-up. Runtime build/typecheck (`node`/`tsc`) not available in this environment; changes are source-level.
