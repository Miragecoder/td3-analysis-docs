  route seam rendering.

## Correction (01:11)

Although no loose `SCENE02P.BIN` exists, the lane bank is embedded in
`SCENE02.DAT`. `SCENE02.LST`'s archive directory maps the hashed filename to
offset `0x1D11A`; the game archive loader resolves this entry before trying the
filesystem. The earlier conclusion that the bank was unavailable was wrong.
The editor now extracts it from the DAT, parses its 127-entry lane index, and
starts route traces from each entity's stored map cell.
